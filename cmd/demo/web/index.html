Sauti Dispatch

Emergency medical dispatch from any phone — no data, no airtime. Kawempe Division, Kampala. MVP demo.

Connecting…

1 Caller phone

Dial *384*1# and answer each screen. Any basic phone can do this: USSD needs no data and no airtime.

2 Responder phones

Village Health Team members and transporters get alerts by SMS. Dial *384*2# to accept or decline.

3 Dispatch board

Live from the operational store, read with the read-only role. A caller's number is never shown.

Dispatch is suspended: no responder is alerted and a coordinator handles every call.

Texts marked PENDING are placeholders awaiting the study's approved wording, shown exactly as the system sends them. SMS goes to a local simulator of the provider; voice calls are not part of this demo. Responders, numbers and places are synthetic.
